## What Iris is, in one sentence

Iris is a local app that watches an AI agent, and stops it when it does something wrong.

Local only, metadata only, no MITM, no bodies, no keys, fail-closed, human in the loop, plain words.

## The product, in plain words

Iris verifies whether the effects an AI agent claimed actually happened, by reading them back. Verdicts: Correct / Incorrect / Unknown. If Incorrect from a read, Iris pauses one Run, redirects one Run, or stops all traffic. If Unknown, Iris holds, does not guess, asks a human.

Filing identification, Tier B free-form, chosen: verifying whether claimed actions occurred and pausing / redirecting / stopping those agents when verification fails. Classes 9 (downloadable software) and 42 (SaaS).
